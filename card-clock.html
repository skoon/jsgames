Card rules & scoring
  1. Interest: 24.9% APR = 2.075% per month. Interest is 2.075% × (opening balance + this month's charges), rounded to the nearest dollar.
  2. Statement: opening balance + automatic charge + selected purchases + interest.
  3. Minimum due: the greater of $30 or 5% of the statement, rounded up to the next dollar and capped at the statement.
  4. Payment: up to $175 per paycheck, capped at the statement. Payment posts after interest.
  5. Late: a payment below the minimum is late. A $35 fee is added after payment, and that month earns no on-time points.
  6. Limit: a purchase is blocked if it would push the projected statement above $4,000.

Score: 350 for on-time payments (35 × 10), 350 for interest efficiency (full at $180 total interest or less), and 300 for a manageable final balance (full at $300 or less). Projections repeat the entered payment or the 5% minimum, with no future purchases.
