How the vault calculates and scores

Deposits. Mira deposits each month from her first-deposit age through her chosen number of years, then stops. Noah deposits from his first-deposit age all the way to 65. Both accounts stay invested until 65.

Interest. The stated annual rate is divided by the compounding periods. That periodic rate is converted to an effective annual rate, then to a monthly-equivalent rate so monthly deposits compare fairly.

periodic = stated rate ÷ periods per year
effective annual = (1 + periodic)periods − 1
monthly equivalent = (1 + effective annual)1/12 − 1

Timing. Each month's deposit is added first; then the monthly-equivalent interest is applied. No withdrawals, taxes, inflation, or fees. All dollars are nominal.

Scoring each jump. 30 points for naming the larger final account, up to 45 points for the combined-balance forecast (full at 2% error or less, then partial through 20%), and 25 points if the combined balance reaches that jump's target. Five jumps: 500 points maximum.
