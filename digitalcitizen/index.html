Digital Citizenship

Privacy and judgement calls, told through the messages on someone else's screen.

  • 01

    Borrowed Phone

    Iris left her phone on the counter and it hasn't stopped buzzing. Read the thread and decide what you owe the person behind it.

    Privacy · Message mystery
  • 02

    Zero-Day Inbox

    Three days on the mailroom desk. Inspect headers, hover the links, and route each message to Safe, Verify or Quarantine — before the fakes learn to use your colleagues' real accounts.

    Phishing · Desktop simulator
  • 03

    Echo Chamber Breakout

    Moderate a trending feed with credibility and engagement pulling opposite ways. Swipe to debunk or amplify, research the image before you believe it, and spend tokens on the bots.

    Media literacy · Deck-builder
  • 04

    The Upstander Protocol

    A ranked lobby turns on one teammate. Answer in public with Direct, Distract or Delegate, switch to their DMs for the private check-in, capture the log and file the report.

    Cyberbullying · Branching RPG
  • 05

    Timeline Architect

    Publish three posts, then jump ten years to read what each one became. Redact the labels, restrict the audience, strip the metadata — or live with the tail.

    Privacy · Timeline puzzle
  • 06

    Remix & Rights

    Three monetised videos, three music licences, one clip each. Read the licence, format the credit, keep the excerpt short — then defend it when the takedown lands.

    Copyright · Tycoon
