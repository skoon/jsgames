Cozy Cafe

A tiny cafe with an extremely strange menu. Regulars want pickled-pear lattes and starlight fogs, poured in exactly the right order.

Tap a ticket to pick a customer, then tap ingredients to build their drink in order.

Keys 1–5 pour bean, milk, honey, mushroom, and stardust. Arrow keys switch customers.

A wrong pour burns patience. Lose three regulars and the cafe closes for good.

Between days, spend coin on upgrades — each one opens another corner of the cafe.
