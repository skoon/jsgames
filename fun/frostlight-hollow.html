Frostlight Hollow

Three stretches of frozen forest, stitched together by lantern light. Leap the gaps, ride the drifting platforms, and follow the glow home.

Arrow keys or A / D run. Space, W, or ↑ jumps — hold it for a higher arc. Touch pads sit below the screen.

Crystal coins are 10 points. Hidden gift boxes are 120, and a few are tucked above the obvious route.

Frost imps and open pits cost a heart. Light a lantern and it becomes your checkpoint.

Reach the glowing portal at the far end of each of the three levels. Run out of hearts and the forest goes quiet.
