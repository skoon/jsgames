Rune Slide

Glowing runes sit in a weighted frame. Roll a row or column, line up three, and the whole board tips — sending every loose rune falling a new way.

Drag a rune sideways to roll its row, or up and down to roll its column. Rows wrap around the frame.

Three runes in a line clear and refund a move. Longer lines score more, and cascades multiply.

Every clear turns gravity a quarter turn. Runes settle that way and fresh ones pour in from the far edge.

Moves are your clock. Tilt gravity or shuffle the frame from the bar below when the board turns stubborn.
