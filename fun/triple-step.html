Triple Step

One screen, six energy tiles, and a clock that only coins can bribe. Every platform carries three jumps.

Arrow keys or A / D run. Space, W, or ↑ jumps. On touch, use the pads below the screen.

Each tile holds three launches. Jump from it a third time and it collapses.

Coins add 2.5 seconds and one point. The clock tops out at 30 seconds.

Collapsed tiles rebuild after five and a half seconds. The timer does not.
