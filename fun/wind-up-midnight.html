Wind-Up Midnight

The toys in the playroom have all been wound. The door has three keyholes. Your candle is small, and the batteries are scattered somewhere in the dark.

Arrow keys or WASD walk. E, space, or the Use pad touches whatever is in front of you. Touch pads sit below the screen.

Six batteries are in the room. One is only visible once the lamp is lit. Batteries pay for the lamp, the music box and the clock.

The music box needs a tune repeated on the rug pads. The clock needs three brass plates thrown up. Each solved device gives you a key.

Wind-up toys patrol the floor. A touch costs a nerve, and three lost nerves end the night. Three keys open the final door.
